LIMUSIC PLUS - AUTOMATIC PER-PC HOSTING (EXPERIMENTAL)

Open Limusic Plus from your desktop or Start menu. Only the app starts.
The application executable is Limusic Plus.exe; its window, tray and About
screen use the same name. Quit your running copy from the tray before upgrading.
Your existing settings, saved rooms and permissions are preserved.
No terminal commands, accounts, router setup, or port forwarding are required.

Discord broadcasting is an optional installer task. Select it if you want to
broadcast from Settings > Discord using your own Discord bot. Listeners only
need to join the Discord voice channel. See docs/DISCORD-VOICE.md in the
included source archive for bot setup.

Host: Open Listen Together, enter your name and choose Host. Your local relay
starts silently and the app establishes automatic encrypted connectivity.
Your session saves automatically and reopens when you host again.
Copy the complete invite and share it once with friends using this Plus build.

Join: Paste your friend's complete invite, join, and wait for their approval.
Choose Save session; the Saved checkmark confirms it. Next time, use Reconnect.
Approval is remembered on your device; you can return freely while the room is
open. A kick revokes this permission. Use stars, Rename and Forget to organize
your saved rooms, including through Manage saved rooms while listening.
Host shows your sessions; Join shows friends' rooms. Availability is checked
automatically, with open rooms first and recent use next. At startup, a popup
offers confirmed-open friends' rooms. Nothing is joined automatically; Not now
dismisses the popup. No popup is shown when no open rooms can be confirmed.
Your friend must be online and hosting. You can host your own session too;
this does not move their session to your PC. Joining starts no local relay.

Close the window to keep listening in the system tray. Choose Quit from the
tray icon to exit the app and stop its relay. A hosted session depends on the
PC that started its relay, including after transferring playback control.

If that PC disconnects, updated apps can still contact the remaining members.
Listen Together offers Keep waiting or Host and invite friends. A volunteer
starts a replacement room; others must choose Join replacement room to move.
Recovery keeps the current track, playback position, play/pause state and up
to 5,000 upcoming tracks. Invited members receive remembered entrance permission
for the replacement room, and its shortcut is saved separately. Everyone needs
this updated Plus build. Invitations last ten minutes while the apps stay open;
old saved room addresses continue to refer to the PC that originally hosted them.

Connections use public Iroh discovery and encrypted relay fallback services.
These have rate limits and no uptime guarantee. Test with your friends before
relying on this experimental build. Everyone streams their own music.

App data: %APPDATA%\com.limusic.plus
Relay reservations: %LOCALAPPDATA%\LimusicRelay\saved-rooms.sqlite3
Back up both folders together. Removing them can invalidate saved invites.
Uninstalling preserves data. Do not share private app databases with friends.
Update with a new Plus installer; original Limusic settings are not imported.
